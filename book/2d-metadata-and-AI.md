# 2d. Metadata and AI 

## Exercise Caution 

- **You hold responsibility for outputs**: You as the researcher are the one responsible for verifying that the outputs from GenAI are correct. 
- **Verify with your supervisor**: Check institutional guidelines or recommendations from your program about which AI tool(s) to use.
- **Disclose your AI use**: If you used GenAI, be transparent about that usage: document which tasks you used it for, which tool and version you used, and keep a record of your prompts. 
- **Protect sensitive data**: Uploading research objects to GenAI is generally not recommended due to privacy concerns. In particular, beware of uploading proprietary information, confidential data, and sensitive data such as medical information.   
  
## Use Generative AI for Metadata 

- You can ask GenAI to help look up metadata standards in your field.  
- You can describe your research workflow and experimental methods, and ask which key details should be included in the metadata.
