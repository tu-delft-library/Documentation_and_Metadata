# Metadata and AI 

## Metadata and Generative AI 
You can ask GenAI to help look up metadata standards in your field.  

You can describe your research workflow and experimental methods, and ask which key details should be included in the metadata.  

! Uploading research objects to GenAI is generally not recommended due to privacy concerns... In particular, beware of uploading sensitive data such as medical information!    

! You as the researcher are the one responsible for verifying that the outputs from GenAI are correct.  

Check institutional guidelines or recommendations from your program about which AI tool(s) to use.  

If you used GenAI, be transparent about that usage: document which tasks you used it for.
